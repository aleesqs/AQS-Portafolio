About

Hi, I'm Alejandra — a digital content producer, storyteller and communicator.

With more than five years of experience in social media strategy, paid campaigns, content creation, audience insights and digital analytics, I've worked with brands, NGOs, creators and corporate teams to turn ideas into communication and content strategies across platforms.

I love staying on top of trends, exploring new formats and understanding how narratives evolve across digital channels. I specialize in developing and executing content strategies for brands, organizations and niche audiences, focused on strengthening their positioning and building relationships with their audiences through horizontal, meaningful communication. I combine creativity, data analysis and strategic vision to turn ideas into tangible results.

Beyond the Work

A strategic, solution-driven mind, focused on turning goals into results.

I love finding the thread that lets a message travel with style across platforms and channels without losing its essence or authenticity. My international experience, including a Master's in Digital Marketing in London, gave me a global, strategic perspective and a competitive view of the digital landscape and its constant transformation.
