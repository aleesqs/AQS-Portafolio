About

Hi, I'm Alejandra — a digital marketing specialist, storyteller, and head-to-tail communicator.

I combine creativity with data-driven thinking to build digital experiences that connect, inspire, and deliver results. With five years of experience across social media strategy, paid campaigns, content creation, audience insights, and digital analytics, I've collaborated with brands, NGOs, creators, and corporate teams to bring ideas to life across multiple platforms. Recently I was involved in a Costco program.

Adaptation is part of who I am professionally — I see using AI as a tool, not a threat, and stay comfortable moving with an industry that keeps changing fast rather than fearing it.
